### CodeRabbit Self-Correction Loop
1. **Fetch Feedback:** Run `coderabbit review --agent`.
2. **Prioritize:** Group findings into [Critical], [Maintainability], and
   [Style].
3. **Apply:** Fix issues starting from [Critical]. Do not ask for permission
   between fixes; proceed autonomously through the list.
4. **Clean up:** Ensure no unused imports or broken types remain after fixes.
5. **Report:** Output a checkbox list of resolved items.
